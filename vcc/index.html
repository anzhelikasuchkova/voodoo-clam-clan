🌤 NOLA in early October: ~85°F, humid, afternoon thunderstorms likely.
Brief but heavy — bring a plan, not a prayer.
  • Swimsuit — the Airbnb has a private pool · pool day is real
  • Shoes — comfortable heels or wedges for cobblestone streets; pack flat backup sandals
  • Heat — blotting papers, mini deodorant, travel fan or cooling mist

  • Emergency kit (shared) — fashion tape, safety pins, blister pads, dry shampoo, spare earring backs
